A [make.fun](https://make.fun) entry for **The Moon Finally Answers Back** — one line, in the Moon's voice, answering four billion years of wishes, howls, and late-night promises.

## The entry

> Four billion years of wishes, howls, and 2 a.m. promises — and not one of you has ever wished me a happy birthday.

(114 characters — inside the 1–240 gate.)

## How it works

`main.py` writes that single line to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$ENTRY_OUT` when set, as a file or as a directory holding `entry.txt`), validating it stays within 1–240 characters first.

```sh
python3 main.py
```
